# HomeLab PDU Mon enclosure (1U, 10 inch, JLCCNC sheet metal)

Everything is generated by `enclosure.py` (parameters at the top).

    cd HomeLab-PDU-Mon
    .venv/bin/python enclosure/enclosure.py

It writes `tray.step lid.step front.step rear.step assembly.step marking.dxf`,
the PNG renders, and prints the interference and clearance report (about 25 s).
Coordinates follow SPEC.md (X = 0 centre line, Y = 0 inner face of the front panel, Z = 0 top of the floor).
STEP files for the four manufactured parts are the bent parts. `assembly.step` also holds placeholder
bodies (HRP-300, PCB, 6 modules and USB-C receptacles, fan, IEC, button, RJ45, PCB screw heads).

## Earthing (mains safety)

The tray is the earth bond. M4 earth hole (4.5 mm) on the floor at X 14, Y 42, next to the IEC inlet.
IEC earth pin goes there with a ring lug, and the HRP FG (terminal 3) goes to the same stud. Use a star washer on each side of the lug.
Anodising is an insulator. Ask JLCCNC to leave 12 mm dia bare around the earth hole (mask), or use chromate conversion on the tray and lid
if they offer it. Lid, panels and tray are bonded through the screws, so on anodised parts use star washers or mask the
contact faces. Do this before putting mains on it. Check continuity from the earth stud to the lid and both panels (< 0.1 ohm).

## JLCCNC DFM notes

- Upload the STEP files. They are the bent parts, JLC unfolds them. If their quote tool wants flat DXF, the flat panels
  (front, rear) are plain plates, and for the tray and lid ask them to unfold with their K-factor.
- Tray and lid bend relief: every flange or tab is cut free of its neighbours (floor tabs have 1.2 x 2.4 relief notches either side,
  wall flanges start 2.4 above the floor and stop 1.2 below the wall top).
- Flange flat length at least 4 t + r = 6.0. Used: wall flanges 8.6, floor tabs 8.8, lid flanges 9.6, lid rear tabs 9.6.
- Hole edge to bend tangent at least 2 t + r = 3.6. Used exactly 3.6 on the wall flange, floor tab and lid flange holes.
  If JLC measures to the bend line rather than the tangent this is 1.2 mm more margin, so fine. If they want more, move the
  hole centres 0.5 mm inwards, there is room (flange end margin is 1.8, the 1.5 t minimum).
- Min hole diameter 3.2, USB-C slot 8 high, all above max(0.5 t, 1).
- Rear marking: `marking.dxf` holds the flat rear panel seen from outside (u = -X) with layer CUT (outline and holes) and layer MARK (port numbers 1 to 6,
  "HomeLab PDU Mon", "Marcos Ashton Iglesias 2026-10-28"). The text is glyph outline (double line) with 0.2 mm polyline width,
  text height 4.5 (numbers), 2.6 and 2.0 (lines). A STEP cannot carry a marking layer, so keep rear.step for the cut and
  use the DXF for the marking. Laser marking on black anodise gives light text. Check that 2.0 mm text is accepted, if not
  raise it to 2.5.
- Anodising and tolerances: sheet cut and bend tolerance is about +-0.2 per feature, bends +-0.5 degree. The 0.3 mm lid-to-wall gap is deliberate.
  Order one spare lid and one spare tray set for the first run.

- Module body is a 12 x 33 x 22 box, its rear face 0.8 mm in front of the rear panel inner face, receptacle shell 8.94 x 3.26 passes through the cutout.

## Decision needed: floor screw heads (floor is 1.2 mm in these files)

All 11 floor holes are modelled with a 90 degree underside countersink, as deep as the sheet allows. The sheet is too thin for flush M3 and M4 heads.
Heads under the floor add to the 43.8 body height.

| Screw | Head dia | Depth for flush | Floor hole | Result at 1.2 mm floor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M2.5 countersunk (2 rear PCB holes) | 5.0 | 1.15 | 2.7 | flush, 0.05 land (very thin edge) |
| M3 countersunk (4 PCB holes) | 6.0 | 1.40 | 3.2 | knife edge, head about 0.2 mm proud |
| M4 countersunk (4 HRP holes and earth) | 8.0 | 1.75 | 4.5 | knife edge, head about 0.55 mm proud |

Option A, accept slightly proud heads: keep the 1.2 mm floor and the countersinks as modelled. Underside protrusion 0.2 mm (M3) and 0.55 mm (M4),
so the real height with heads is 43.8 + 0.55 = 44.35 at the HRP screws. Cheapest, but the countersink has no land, so ask JLCCNC whether they accept it.
Using M3 and M4 low-head cap screws instead is worse (1.65 to 2.8 mm proud).

Option B, dimples (extruded or formed countersinks, in the 1.2 mm sheet): head sits flush or nearly so because the cone is pressed down into the sheet,
and the dimple depth is not limited by the sheet thickness. Needs JLCCNC to confirm they can form it in 1.2 mm 5052. Body height stays 43.8.
The part would need the dimple formed on the underside, and the floor above gets a raised cone of about 0.5 to 0.8 mm in the M3 and M4 places,
which must clear the HRP and PCB standoff seats (the standoff and HRP sit on the raised cone, so check their flatness).

Option C, 1.5 mm floor (not applied): M3 becomes flush (needs 1.4), M4 is still 0.25 mm proud (needs 1.75). The floor is 0.3 mm thicker. Either the body becomes 44.1
(over the 43.8 limit and the 44.0 front panel) or the interior drops to 41.1 and the HRP (41.0) has 0.1 mm to the lid, too tight.

My suggestion: ask JLCCNC about dimples first, fall back to A.
